# Asset credits

All assets below are used under their stated licenses. Nothing here is from Rocket League itself.

## 3D models
- **Car body and wheel** (`models/car.glb`, `models/wheel.glb`, `models/Textures/colormap.png`): Kenney, *Car Kit* — https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit — CC0 1.0.

## Sounds
- **Engine loops** (`sounds/engine_low.wav`, `sounds/engine_high.wav`): *Racing car engine sound loops* — https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops — CC0 1.0.
- **Boost roar / ignition, goal explosion, big pad pickup** (`boost_loop.ogg`, `boost_ignite.ogg`, `goal_crunch.ogg`, `goal_boom.ogg`, `pad_big.ogg`): Kenney, *Sci-fi Sounds* — https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds — CC0 1.0.
- **Ball hits, car hits, landings** (`ball_hit_*.ogg`, `car_hit_*.ogg`, `land_*.ogg`): Kenney, *Impact Sounds* — https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds — CC0 1.0.
- **Small pad pickup, countdown tick and go** (`pad_small.ogg`, `count_tick.ogg`, `count_go.ogg`): Kenney, *Interface Sounds* — https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds — CC0 1.0.

Everything else (whooshes, skid, wind, beeps, horn) is synthesised at runtime with WebAudio. Every sample above is CC0, so no attribution is required; it is recorded here anyway.

There is deliberately no crowd or stadium ambience: sound is only ever a response to something the player did.

## Daylight stadium artwork

The stadium bowl, seating, spectators, roof trusses, banners, sky/cloud texture, turf, safety mesh, scoreboard and added car details are original procedural geometry and Canvas textures implemented in this repository. The Soccar reference was inspected visually for general presentation; no reference source, models, textures, sounds, branding or other assets were copied.
